---

Notion Calendar is a beautifully designed calendar app that brings your schedule and your Notion workspace together.

Installing this app adds Notion Calendar to your Cal.diy app store and links you straight to [notion.com/product/calendar](https://www.notion.com/product/calendar), where you can download the app and get started. This is a link-only integration — it does not require OAuth, API keys, or access to your Cal.diy account.

- Manage your day with a fast, keyboard-first calendar
- See your Notion databases and events side by side
- Share availability and send scheduling links people love
- Works across macOS, Windows, web, and mobile
